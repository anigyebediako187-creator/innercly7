INNERCLY V137 — PLATFORM ROLES QUICK REFERENCE

FOUR PRIVATE TEAMS

EXECUTIVE
- Administrator: assigned Executive workspace; operational oversight and
  explicitly listed community capabilities. Cannot assign/revoke platform roles,
  grant badges, alter Owner controls, or use Owner-only finance controls.
- Owner: @innercly_owner; all four teams and all platform permissions. Protected.

COMMUNITY & SAFETY
- Community Manager: manage public community pages and platform events.
- Senior Moderator: view/resolve reports and escalate serious cases.
- Moderator: view and resolve reports.

TECHNICAL
- Developer Lead: technical diagnostics, issue submission/review, QA review.
- Web Developer: technical diagnostics and issue submission/review.
- QA Lead: diagnostics, issue submission/review, QA review.
- QA Tester: diagnostics and issue submission. Cannot review the whole queue.

SUPPORT & CREATORS
- Support Agent: private team-area access. A ticket queue is not in this build.
- Creator: Creator Dashboard/analytics. Not a staff moderation or admin role.

ACCESS RULES
- Only the Owner assigns/revokes platform roles.
- Users can hold multiple roles. Their visible team areas are derived from those
  roles; a role in one team does not grant a different team's permissions.
- Normal Members do not see Staff Centre. Server APIs also enforce each
  permission and return 403 when access is not granted.
- Staff permissions are not public profile data. Private messages, password
  hashes, payment secrets, and database credentials are not exposed to staff.
- Group-specific roles, verification badges, Premium, and Verified Creators
  invitations are separate systems.
- Owner changes are recorded in the platform role audit history.
