INNERCLY V137 — PROFILE EDITING & STAFF ACCESS

STAFF CENTRE ACCESS
1. Sign in to InnerCly.
2. Open the account menu in the top-right corner.
3. Accounts with platform roles will see Staff Centre.
4. The Owner also sees Roles & Permissions.
5. The same staff links remain available in the left navigation and mobile More menu.
6. Staff only see the team areas granted to them. Ordinary Members do not see staff links and cannot open staff APIs.

PROFILE EDITING
1. Open your own Profile.
2. Press Edit Profile, or use the camera button to change your profile picture.
3. You can also open the account menu and select Edit profile.
4. Change display name, username, bio, or profile picture and press Save profile.
5. The server confirms the update. Your bio is retained across sign-in and refresh.

V137 FIXES
- Profile bio is preserved through authentication normalization.
- Desktop and mobile profile edit entry points share the same delegated handler.
- Profile updates save independently of the feed refresh, so a temporary feed refresh failure does not misreport the saved profile.
- Owner/staff navigation is derived from the authenticated Owner role and assigned platform roles, including a fallback when staff_teams is missing from a stale client payload.

This V137 package contains the full project and both unchanged V40/V80 master backups. It does not alter the hosted service until deployed.
