HOW VERIFIED CREATORS WORK IN INNERCLY

Verified Creators is a private, invite-only space. It is separate from the
verification badge shown beside an account name.

1. An account can have a verification badge (blue, purple, gold, or green)
   without automatically being a member of Verified Creators.
2. The InnerCly Owner controls invitations from the Owner-only Verified
   Creators invitation controls.
3. An invited account can open the Verified Creators area, view its private
   posts, and publish text/photo/video posts there.
4. The Owner can remove an invitation. When access is removed, the account
   can no longer open or publish in that private area.
5. The Owner account can access the area without a separate invitation.

The badges communicate account status/identity; the invitation controls access
to the private creator community. They are two different systems.
